Dining Hall Etiquette

  • Scan QR attendance at the turnstile before approaching counters.
  • Avoid food wastage: Take only portions you can finish.
  • Return trays and cutlery cleanly to the conveyor belt station.
  • Do not take mess utensils or glassware outside dining halls.
  • For acute concerns, use the File a Complaint form with photos.
